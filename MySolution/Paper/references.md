# References

> **Note for the writer.** The drafted sections use a *mixed* citation style: source-paper references are cited as `[N]` using the numbering of Maltezou-Papastylianou et al. ([1] for the source paper itself, [7], [10], [14]–[17] for source-paper refs); new references introduced in §II are cited in author-year form (e.g., "Ganin et al., 2016"). The unified bibliography below is numbered sequentially `[1]`–`[35]` in **IEEE conference style**. When pasting the paper into LaTeX or Word, run a find-and-replace to convert every author-year citation to its matching `[N]` from this list. Entries are grouped by topic for ease of cross-reference; in the final paper they appear as one flat numbered list in citation order.
